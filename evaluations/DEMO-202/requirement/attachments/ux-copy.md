# UX copy — Guest enquiries (approved by Content, v2)

## Contact form

- Section heading: **Send Us a Message**
- Field labels: **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Subject**, **Message** (visible and programmatically associated)
- Button: **Submit**

## Confirmation (replaces the form after a successful submit)

- Heading: `Thanks for getting in touch <Name>!`  (the guest's name as typed, no comma)
- Body: `We'll get back to you about` / `<Subject>` / `as soon as possible.` (may be shown on separate lines)

## Rooms list ("Our Rooms")

- Each card shows the room **type** as its heading and the price as `£<roomPrice> per night`.
- Card image alternative text: `<Type> Room` — e.g. `Single Room`, `Double Room`, `Suite Room`.
