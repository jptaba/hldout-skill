# Pricing rules — web shop (v2.3, owner: Finance)

## Display
- All prices are shown in USD with two decimals, prefixed with `$` (e.g. `$29.99`).

## Item total
- Item total = sum of the unit prices of every item in the cart (quantity 1 per product).

## Tax
- Sales tax is **10%** of the item total.
- Tax is rounded half-up to 2 decimals.

## Total
- Total = Item total + Tax, shown with two decimals.

## Shipping
- Shipping is free for all orders and is not added to the total.
